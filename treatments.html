General Dentistry

The Foundation of a Healthy Smile

Regular dental care is the cornerstone of long-term oral health. At Dental Design Studio, our general dentistry services go far beyond a routine check-up — we offer comprehensive care that protects and maintains your teeth for life.

Every examination includes an oral cancer screening, gum disease assessment, toothwear and caries risk analysis, and a digital photographic tour of your mouth — giving you a full picture of your dental health.

Dental Implants

Permanent Solutions for Missing Teeth

Dental implants are the gold standard solution for replacing missing teeth. They look, feel and function just like natural teeth, and with proper care they can last a lifetime.

Our implant surgeon Ferhan Ahmed is a specialist in complex implant cases. From single-tooth replacements to full arch All-on-4/5 restorations, we have the expertise to give you back your smile and confidence.

  • Single tooth implants from £2,200
  • Full arch All-on-4/5 implant bridges
  • Implant-supported dentures and bridges
  • IV sedation available for nervous patients

Nervous Patients

You're in Safe, Gentle Hands

We understand that visiting the dentist can be daunting. At Dental Design Studio, we have a deeply experienced team who specialise in caring for nervous and anxious patients.

Our hygienist Priyanka is also a qualified hypnotherapist — uniquely placed to help patients overcome dental phobia, needle anxiety and strong gag reflex. We also offer IV conscious sedation so you can receive treatment in a completely relaxed, comfortable state.

  • Free treatment coordinator consultation — no obligation
  • Friendly, unhurried appointments — always at your pace
  • Hypnotherapy support available for dental phobia
  • IV conscious sedation — feel completely at ease
  • Calm, welcoming environment with TVs, Wi-Fi and refreshments
