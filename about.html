Our Story

A Practice Built on Excellence

Dental Design Studio is a top-rated private dental practice located in Edgworth, Bolton — less than 20 minutes from Bolton town centre and easily accessible from across Greater Manchester.

Founded by Dr Riaz Mitha, the practice has been serving the local community since 2004. Our philosophy is simple: provide outstanding dental care in a calm, welcoming environment, using the very latest techniques and materials.

We specialise in minimally invasive dentistry — improving your smile with either minimal or no tooth preparation. Our attention to detail and genuine commitment to each patient's individual goals is what sets us apart.

CQC regulated and continuously monitored for your safety

GDC registered team — every member is fully qualified and accountable

Latest technology, treatments and materials used throughout

Modern, air-conditioned surgeries with LCD TVs, free Wi-Fi and disabled access

Approved Denplan and Invisalign provider

Our Team

The People Behind Your Smile

Get to know the passionate professionals who make Dental Design Studio so special.

Dr Riaz Mitha

Principal Dentist & Practice Owner
BDS (Manchester), MSc Advanced Restorative & Cosmetic Dentistry (UCLan)
GDC No: 81943

Dr Riaz Mitha qualified from the University of Manchester in 2003 and has been practising in Bolton since 2004. With almost 20 years of extensive clinical experience, he holds a Masters degree in Advanced Restorative and Cosmetic Dentistry from the University of Central Lancashire.

Riaz has trained with Dr Larry Rosenthal, director of the world-renowned Rosenthal Institute in New York — whose clients include Catherine Zeta Jones, Michael Douglas and Donald Trump. This world-class training, combined with his natural eye for aesthetics, enables Riaz to create truly stunning smile transformations.

  • Specialist interest in orthodontics including Invisalign and Six Month Smiles
  • Expert in composite bonding, porcelain veneers and smile design
  • Implant surgeon with extensive restorative implant experience
  • Committed to continuing professional development and the latest techniques

Ferhan Ahmed

Implant Surgeon
BDS (Glasg), MFDS RCPS (Glasg), MBChB (Liv)
GDC No: 85401

Ferhan is a highly experienced oral surgeon specialising in dental implants and conscious IV sedation. Trained under leading surgeons in Scotland and the North of England, he brings exceptional skill and calm expertise to even the most complex implant cases.

  • Member, Association of Dental Implantology
  • Member, British Association of Oral Surgeons
  • Member, Society for Advancement of Anaesthesia in Dentistry
  • Specialist in single, multiple and full arch implant solutions

Priyanka Gupta

Dental Hygienist & Hypnotherapist
Dental Hygienist — GDC Registered
GDC No: 266134

Priyanka is a qualified dental hygienist and hypnotherapist who specialises in the care of anxious and nervous patients. Her unique dual qualification enables her to help patients overcome dental phobia, needle anxiety, and strong gag reflex, making their experience as calm and comfortable as possible.

  • Specialist support for nervous patients and dental phobia
  • Gentle, patient-centred approach — multiple 5-star reviews
  • Experienced in periodontal (gum disease) management
